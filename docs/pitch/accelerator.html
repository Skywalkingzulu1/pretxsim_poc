Problem

01Intent is opaque before settlement — on every ledger

On Canton: atomic but blind

Commits are atomic — yet intent is opaque prior to settlement. A payer staring at a contract ID can't answer "what will this do, and is it inside my mandate?" Public RPC dry-runs leak enterprise sub-transaction privacy metadata to external nodes — unacceptable for treasuries, RWA issuers, and agent operators.

On EVM: blind and drained

Wallet-drainer phishing stole $494M in 2024 (+67% YoY). And every serious simulation fix — Blockaid ($83M raised), Tenderly — executes on vendor servers, leaking trade flow and treasury moves. (Alchemy killed its sim APIs in Sept 2026.)

The gap: no deterministic, privacy-preserving pre-execution layer exists for either world. PreviewGate fills it on Canton; PreTxSim fills it on EVM.
Solution · two rails, one engine

02Deterministic rehearsal before anything commits

The same thesis on both ledgers: show exactly what will happen, gate what shouldn't — with zero privacy leakage.

Canton / Daml — PreviewGate (flagship)

  • Non-consuming Preview choices evaluate owner policy bounds (GatedPayment) in-memory — no state commits
  • In-policy settles; out-of-policy escalates to single-use owner grants
  • Every claim proven by Daml Script; one-command verify.sh

EVM / cross-chain — PreTxSim (expansion)

  • Air-gapped :8545 proxy: local revm traces for bridge & agent flows
  • RiskInspector + offline Ollama brief; --ci JSON reports that fail builds
  • Wallets, bots, agents, CI — zero integration cost
