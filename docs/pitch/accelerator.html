PreTxSim · Accelerator Pitch

The local security layer for every onchain transaction.

PreTxSim is an open-source, air-gapped transaction simulation proxy. It rehearses every transaction against live state and blocks the dangerous ones — with nothing ever leaving the user's machine. The privacy-preserving alternative to a market dominated by cloud APIs.

Problem

01Signing is blind — and the fixes all phone home

Losses are growing

Wallet-drainer phishing stole $494M in 2024 (+67% YoY, 332k victims). One malicious approval or EIP-7702 delegation empties a wallet. Agents moving money onchain multiply the attack surface.

Incumbents see your flow

Blockaid ($83M raised), Tenderly, and wallets' in-house sims all execute on vendor servers. Transaction intent — trade flow, treasury moves, agent behavior — leaks to a third party. Enterprises and funds can't accept that. (Alchemy killed its sim APIs in Sept 2026.)

The gap: no local-first, open, programmable simulation layer exists. Security is a vendor API call — it should be infrastructure you run.
Solution

02A security proxy at the signing moment

Point anything that signs at http://127.0.0.1:8545. PreTxSim intercepts eth_sendTransaction, simulates it locally against live state, produces a risk brief, and gates broadcast on your approval — or your pipeline's.

For users

Wallet guardrail: malicious txs refused before broadcast, in plain language.

For agents & bots

Mandatory sandbox: autonomous payments can't drain what simulation blocks.

For teams

--ci mode: JSON risk reports that fail the build on BLOCK findings.
